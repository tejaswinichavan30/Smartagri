# ICAR Agricultural Guidelines: Crop Management & Soil Fertility (NPK)

## 1. Rice (Paddy)
- **Season & Climate**: Kharif crop. Requires high temperatures (20°C - 37°C), prolonged sunshine, and high humidity (>70%).
- **Soil Requirement**: Clayey or clay-loam soils with high water retention capacity. Ideal soil pH is 5.5 to 6.5.
- **Recommended NPK Ratio**: 100-120 kg N, 50-60 kg P2O5, 40-50 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Fertilizer Application Schedule**:
  - Basal Application (at transplanting): 50% Nitrogen, 100% Phosphorus, 50% Potassium.
  - Tillering Stage (20-25 days after transplanting): 25% Nitrogen top-dressing.
  - Panicle Initiation Stage (40-45 days after transplanting): 25% Nitrogen + remaining 50% Potassium.
  - Zinc Deficiency Management: Apply 25 kg Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4) per hectare basally or foliar spray 0.5% ZnSO4 + 0.25% lime.
- **Water Management**: Maintain 2-5 cm standing water during vegetative and flowering stages. Drain water 10-15 days before harvest.

## 2. Wheat
- **Season & Climate**: Rabi crop. Requires cool weather during vegetative growth (12°C - 22°C) and warm, sunny weather during grain filling (20°C - 25°C).
- **Soil Requirement**: Well-drained fertile loamy to clay-loam soils. Ideal pH is 6.0 to 7.5.
- **Recommended NPK Ratio**: 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Fertilizer Application Schedule**:
  - Basal: 50% N + 100% P + 100% K at sowing.
  - First Irrigation (Crown Root Initiation - CRI at 20-25 days): Top dress 25% N.
  - Second Irrigation (Booting/Flowering stage at 40-45 days): Top dress remaining 25% N.
- **Critical Irrigation Stages**: Crown Root Initiation (CRI at 21 days), Tillering (45 days), Late Jointing (65 days), Flowering (85 days), Milking (105 days), Dough stage (120 days).

---

## 3. Maize (Corn)
- **Season & Climate**: Kharif / Rabi / Zaid. Warm season crop needing 21°C to 30°C. Highly sensitive to waterlogging and frost.
- **Soil Requirement**: Deep, fertile, well-drained sandy loam to silty loam. pH 5.5 to 7.5.
- **Recommended NPK Ratio**: 120-150 kg N, 60-75 kg P2O5, 40-50 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Application Tips**:
  - Basal: 33% N, full P, full K.
  - Knee-high stage (30-35 days): 33% N.
  - Tasseling stage (50-55 days): 34% N.

---

## 4. Cotton
- **Season & Climate**: Kharif crop. Requires minimum 180-200 frost-free days. Optimum temperature is 21°C to 35°C.
- **Soil Requirement**: Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) with good moisture retention. pH 6.0 to 8.0.
- **Recommended NPK Ratio**: 100-120 kg N, 50-60 kg P2O5, 50-60 kg K2O per hectare for hybrid/Bt cotton.
- **Micronutrient Management**: Apply Boron (0.1% Borax foliar spray) and Magnesium Sulfate (1%) at flowering to prevent leaf reddening and square dropping.

---

## 5. Chickpea (Gram) & Pulses
- **Season & Climate**: Rabi pulse. Semi-arid conditions with 15°C to 25°C. Highly sensitive to excess moisture and cloudy weather.
- **Soil Requirement**: Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam soils. pH 6.0 to 7.5.
- **Recommended NPK Ratio**: 20-25 kg N, 50-60 kg P2O5, 20 kg K2O per hectare. Pulses fix atmospheric nitrogen via Rhizobium nodules; hence low N is needed as a starter dose.
- **Bio-fertilizers**: Inoculate seeds with *Rhizobium* culture (200 g/10 kg seed) and Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) before sowing.

---

## 6. Tomato & Vegetable Crops
- **Climate & Soil**: Warm season crop. Ideal temperature 20°C to 28°C. Well-drained sandy loam or clay loam rich in organic matter. pH 6.0 to 7.0.
- **Recommended NPK Ratio**: 150-180 kg N, 100-120 kg P2O5, 100-150 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Calcium & Magnesium**: Apply Calcium Nitrate (0.5% spray) at fruiting stage to prevent Blossom End Rot (BER). Apply 25 kg/ha Magnesium Sulfate for leaf chlorosis prevention.

---

## 7. Mango (Mangifera indica) - Per Tree & Per Acre Nutrition
- **Climate & Soil**: Tropical and subtropical fruit. Ideal temperature 24°C to 30°C. Deep, well-drained loamy to alluvial soils. pH 6.0 to 7.5.
- **Organic Manure (FYM)**:
  - 1–3 year old tree: 10–15 kg FYM + 500g Neem cake per tree.
  - 4–9 year old tree: 25–40 kg FYM + 1.5 kg Neem cake per tree.
  - Mature bearing tree (10+ years): 50–60 kg well-decomposed FYM + 2 kg Neem cake applied in a circular ring/basin 1.5–2m away from the trunk during June–July (monsoon).
- **Recommended NPK Dose per Tree (by Tree Age)**:
  - Year 1: 100g N : 50g P2O5 : 100g K2O
  - Year 2 to 9: Increase N by 100g, P by 50g, K by 100g per year.
  - Bearing tree (10+ years): **1000g N (approx 2.2 kg Urea) : 500g P2O5 (approx 3.1 kg Single Super Phosphate - SSP) : 1000g K2O (approx 1.7 kg Muriate of Potash - MOP)** per tree per year.
- **Application Schedule & Timing**:
  - **1st Split (July–August / Post-Harvest Monsoon)**: Apply 50% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus (SSP) + 50% Potassium (MOP) along with FYM to promote post-harvest vegetative flush.
  - **2nd Split (September–October / Pre-Flowering)**: Apply remaining 50% Nitrogen + 50% Potassium to support flower bud differentiation.
- **Micronutrients & Foliar Sprays for Heavy Fruit Set**:
  - **Flower Induction (Oct–Nov)**: Foliar spray of Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) @ 10g/L or 0:52:34 @ 5g/L.
  - **Fruit Retention & Quality (Pea to Marble Stage)**: Spray Solubor / Borax @ 1.5g/L + Zinc Sulfate @ 2g/L + 0:0:50 @ 5g/L to eliminate fruit drop and maximize fruit size and TSS (sweetness).
