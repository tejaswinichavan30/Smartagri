# Crop Irrigation Schedules, Critical Stages and Water Management Guide

## 1. Maize (Corn / मक्का / ಮೆಕ್ಕೆಜೋಳ) Irrigation Schedule
### Irrigation Interval:
- **Sandy / Light Red Soil**: Every 6 to 8 days.
- **Loamy / Heavy Black Soil**: Every 10 to 12 days.
- **Summer / Spring Crop**: Every 5 to 7 days due to high evaporation.
- **Kharif (Monsoon) Crop**: Rainfed under normal rainfall; provide life-saving irrigation during 10-15 day dry spells.

### 4 Most Critical Stages for Maize Irrigation:
1. **Knee-High Stage (30 - 35 days after sowing)**: Rapid vegetative growth.
2. **Tasseling Stage (45 - 50 days)**: Male flower emergence (Most critical stage — water stress reduces pollination).
3. **Silking Stage (60 - 65 days)**: Cob and silk development for grain formation.
4. **Milking / Grain Filling Stage (75 - 80 days)**: Enhances grain weight, size and starch quality.
- **Water Management Tip**: Avoid waterlogging at seedling stage; ensure proper field drainage.

---

## 2. Paddy / Rice (धान / ಭತ್ತ) Irrigation Schedule
- **Transplanting to Tillering (0-30 days)**: Maintain shallow standing water (2-3 cm).
- **Panicle Initiation & Flowering (45-75 days)**: Maintain 5 cm standing water (critical stage).
- **Grain Hardening (> 80 days)**: Drain water 10-12 days before harvest.

---

## 3. Cotton (कपास / ಹತ್ತಿ) Irrigation Schedule
- **Interval**: Light soils: every 10-12 days; Black soils: every 15-20 days.
- **Critical Stages**: Square formation (40-45 days), Flowering (60-70 days), Boll development (80-110 days).

---

## 4. Sugarcane (गन्ना / ಕಬ್ಬು) Irrigation Schedule
- **Germination (0-30 days)**: Every 7-10 days.
- **Formative / Tillering (30-120 days)**: Every 8-10 days (highest water requirement).
- **Grand Growth (120-270 days)**: Every 10-15 days.
- **Maturity (270-360 days)**: Every 20 days. Stop irrigation 15 days before harvest to increase brix/sugar content.

---

## 5. Tomato & Vegetables (टमाटर / ತರಕಾರಿ)
- **Drip Irrigation**: Daily 1.5 to 2.5 hours (morning).
- **Surface Irrigation**: Light sandy soil: every 4-6 days; Heavy soil: every 8-10 days.
- **Critical Stages**: Flowering, Fruit setting, and Fruit development.
