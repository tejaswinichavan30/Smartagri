# Comprehensive Plant Disease Diagnosis & Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

## 1. Tomato Diseases

### A. Tomato Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*)
- **Symptoms**: Dark brown to black concentric rings ("target board" appearance) on older leaves. Leaves turn yellow and drop prematurely.
- **Organic & Biological Control**:
  - Spray *Trichoderma harzianum* or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (5-10 g/L water) at early stages.
  - Foliar application of Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) or 1500 ppm Neem Oil (3-5 ml/L water).
  - Practice crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops (avoid planting after potato, brinjal, or chilli).
- **Chemical Control**:
  - Preventive spray: Mancozeb 75 WP (2.5 g/L) or Copper Oxychloride 50 WP (3.0 g/L).
  - Curative/Systemic spray: Azoxystrobin 23 SC (1 ml/L) or Difenoconazole 25 EC (1 ml/L) or Tebuconazole 25.9 EC (1.5 ml/L).

### B. Tomato Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked dark brown to purple irregular lesions on leaves with white fuzzy fungal growth on the underside during humid/foggy weather.
- **Organic Treatment**: Copper Hydroxide spray, removal and destruction of infected plant debris.
- **Chemical Control**: Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil MZ @ 2.5 g/L) or Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (2 g/L).

### C. Tomato Leaf Mold (*Passalora fulva*)
- **Symptoms**: Pale greenish-yellow spots on upper leaf surface, with olive-green to brown velvety fungal growth underneath.
- **Management**: Improve greenhouse/field air circulation, avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation, spray Chlorothalonil 75 WP (2 g/L).

### D. Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (TYLCV)
- **Vector**: Transmitted by Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*).
- **Symptoms**: Severe stunting, upward leaf curling, yellowing of leaf margins, reduced fruit set.
- **Control**: Install yellow sticky traps (15-20 traps/acre). Spray Imidacloprid 17.8 SL (0.5 ml/L) or Thiamethoxam 25 WG (0.3 g/L) or Acetamiprid 20 SP (0.4 g/L) to control whitefly vector.

## 2. Potato Diseases

### A. Potato Early Blight & Late Blight
- **Early Blight**: Mancozeb 75 WP (2.5 g/L) or Chlorothalonil 75 WP (2 g/L).
- **Late Blight**: Dimethomorph 50 WP (1 g/L) + Mancozeb (2 g/L) or Fenamidone 10% + Mancozeb 50% WG (Sectin @ 2.5 g/L).
- **Preventive Care**: Use certified disease-free seed tubers. High-ridging/earthing up to prevent tuber infection.

## 3. Rice (Paddy) Diseases & Pests

### A. Rice Blast (*Magnaporthe oryzae*)
- **Symptoms**: Spindle-shaped/diamond-shaped lesions with grayish-white centers and brownish margins on leaves and neck rot on panicles.
- **Control**: Seed treatment with Tricyclazole 75 WP (2 g/kg seed). Foliar spray of Tricyclazole 75 WP (0.6 g/L) or Isoprothiolane 40 EC (1.5 ml/L) or Kasugamycin 3% SL (2 ml/L).

### B. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH) (*Nilaparvata lugens*)
- **Symptoms**: "Hopper burn" - circular patches of dried yellow-brown crops due to sap sucking at the base of plants.
- **Control**: Drain water from field for 3-4 days (alternate wetting and drying). Avoid excessive Nitrogen fertilizer. Spray Pymetrozine 50 WG (0.6 g/L) or Dinotefuran 20 SG (0.4 g/L) or Triflumuron 480 SC.

## 4. Corn (Maize) Diseases & Pests

### A. Northern Leaf Blight (*Exserohilum turcicum*)
- **Symptoms**: Long, elliptical grayish-green or tan lesions on leaves.
- **Control**: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP (2.5 g/L) or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC (1 ml/L).

### B. Fall Armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*)
- **Symptoms**: Ragged leaf feeding with sawdust-like frass in leaf whorls, damaged growing points.
- **Control**:
  - Pheromone traps @ 5 per acre.
  - Biological: *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) @ 2 g/L or *Metarhizium anisopliae* @ 5 g/L.
  - Chemical: Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG (0.4 g/L) or Spinetoram 11.7 SC (0.5 ml/L) or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC (0.4 ml/L).

---

## 5. Wheat Rusts (Brown / Yellow / Black Rust)
- **Symptoms**: Yellowish-orange (Stripe/Yellow Rust) or reddish-brown (Leaf/Brown Rust) powdery pustules on leaves.
- **Management**: Grow resistant cultivars (e.g., HD-2967, DBW-187, DBW-222). Spray Propiconazole 25 EC (Tilt @ 1 ml/L) or Tebuconazole 25.9 EC (1.5 ml/L) immediately upon noticing first pustules.
