# Karnataka Agricultural Crop Calendar, Sowing Seasons and Varieties

## Kharif Season (June - July / Monsoon Sowing)
In Karnataka, the South-West Monsoon begins in early June. This is the primary cultivation season (Kharif/Mungaru).

### Top Suitable Crops for June Sowing in Karnataka:
1. **Paddy / Rice (ಭತ್ತ)**:
   - Sowing/Transplanting: June to July.
   - Recommended Zones: Coastal (Udupi, Dakshina Kannada, Uttara Kannada), Malnad (Shivamogga, Chikkamagaluru, Kodagu), and canal-irrigated zones (Mandya, Mysuru, Raichur, Ballari).
   - High-yielding Varieties: Jyothi, Jaya, IR-64, Tanu, KRH-4, MTU-1001, Gangavathi Sona.
   - Fertilizer: 100 kg N, 50 kg P2O5, 50 kg K2O per hectare + 25 kg Zinc Sulfate.

2. **Maize / Corn (ಮೆಕ್ಕೆಜೋಳ)**:
   - Sowing: June - July.
   - Major Districts: Davanagere, Haveri, Belagavi, Ballari, Chikkamagaluru, Chitradurga.
   - Varieties: Deccan-103, CP-818, Kargil 900M, Pioneer 3396.
   - Sowing Tip: Treat seeds with Imidacloprid (4ml/kg) to protect against Fall Armyworm.

3. **Ragi / Finger Millet (ರಾಗಿ)**:
   - Sowing: June - July (Direct seeding) / July - August (Transplanting).
   - Major Districts: Tumakuru, Hassan, Bengaluru Rural, Ramanagara, Kolar, Chikkaballapura, Mandya.
   - Varieties: GPU-28, MR-1, ML-365, GPU-48, KMR-301.
   - Benefits: Extremely drought-tolerant, ideal for dryland red soils.

4. **Cotton (ಹತ್ತಿ)**:
   - Sowing: Early June with first monsoon showers.
   - Major Districts: Raichur, Dharwad, Haveri, Belagavi, Ballari, Kalaburagi, Mysuru.
   - Soil: Deep black cotton soil (Vertisols) with good moisture retention.
   - Varieties: Bunny Bt, RCH-2, Mallika Bt, DHH-11.

5. **Redgram / Tur / Pigeonpea (ತೊಗರಿ)**:
   - Sowing: June - early July.
   - Major Districts: Kalaburagi (The Tur Bowl of Karnataka), Bidar, Yadgir, Raichur.
   - Varieties: GRG-811, BS-1, TS-3R, Asha (ICPL-87119).
   - Intercropping: Redgram + Soybean (1:2) or Redgram + Groundnut (1:5).

6. **Groundnut / Peanut (ನೆಲಗಡಲೆ / ಶೇಂಗಾ)**:
   - Sowing: June - July.
   - Major Districts: Tumakuru, Chitradurga, Kolar, Chikkaballapura, Dharwad, Belagavi.
   - Varieties: TMV-2, JL-24, GPBD-4, KDG-128.
   - Requirement: Gypsum 500 kg/ha at 30-40 days for pod filling.

7. **Soybean (ಸೋಯಾಬೀನ್)**:
   - Sowing: June 15 to July 10.
   - Major Districts: Belagavi, Bidar, Dharwad, Haveri.
   - Varieties: JS-335, JS-9305, DSb-21.

8. **Greengram & Blackgram (ಹೆಸರು ಮತ್ತು ಉದ್ದು)**:
   - Sowing: Early June (first monsoon rains).
   - Short duration: 65 to 75 days harvest.
   - Varieties: Selection-4, DGGV-2 (Greengram); Rashmi, TAU-1 (Blackgram).

9. **Sugarcane (ಕಬ್ಬು)**:
   - Planting: Adsali (June-July) or Eksali (Oct-Feb).
   - Major Districts: Belagavi, Mandya, Bagalkot, Mysuru, Vijayapura.
   - Varieties: Co-86032 (Nayana), Co-62175, Co-92005.

10. **Vegetables & Spices (ತರಕಾರಿ ಮತ್ತು ಸಂಬಾರ ಬೆಳೆಗಳು)**:
    - Tomato (Arka Rakshak, Shivam), Chilli (Byadgi, Sitara), Onion (Arka Kalyan, Bhima Super), Ginger, Turmeric.

## Rabi Season (October - November / Winter Sowing)
- **Chickpea (Bengal Gram / ಕಡಲೆ)**: JG-11, JAKI-9218, Annigeri-1 (Kalaburagi, Dharwad, Vijayapura).
- **Wheat (ಗೋಧಿ)**: DWR-162, DWR-2006, UAS-304 (Northern Karnataka).
- **Rabi Jowar (ಹಿಂಗಾರು ಜೋಳ)**: M 35-1 (Maldandi), BJV-44.
- **Sunflower (ಸೂರ್ಯಕಾಂತಿ)**: KBSH-1, KBSH-44, RSFH-130.

---

## Summer / Zaid Season (February - March / Irrigated)
- **Watermelon & Muskmelon**: February planting for high summer market prices.
- **Summer Groundnut**: TMV-2, GPBD-4 with drip irrigation.
- **Summer Pulses & Vegetables**: Cucumber, Bottle Gourd, Okra.
