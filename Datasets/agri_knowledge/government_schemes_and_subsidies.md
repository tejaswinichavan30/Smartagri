# Indian Government Agricultural Schemes, Subsidies & Farmer Support

## 1. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- **Objective**: Direct income support to landholding farmer families across India.
- **Benefit**: ₹6,000 per year transferred directly into Aadhaar-linked bank accounts in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months.
- **Eligibility**: All landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their name.
- **Registration**: Through pmkisan.gov.in portal or CSC (Common Service Centers) with Aadhaar card, land ownership documents, and bank passbook.

---

## 2. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) - Crop Insurance
- **Objective**: Comprehensive financial security against crop loss due to non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rains, pest attacks, post-harvest losses).
- **Premium Rates for Farmers**:
  - Kharif crops: Maximum 2% of the sum insured.
  - Rabi crops: Maximum 1.5% of the sum insured.
  - Commercial & Horticultural crops: Maximum 5% of the sum insured.
  - Remaining premium subsidy is shared 50:50 between Central and State Governments.
- **Claim Process**: Report crop damage within 72 hours of occurrence via the Crop Insurance App, Toll-Free number (14447), or local Agriculture Officer.

---

## 3. Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) - Per Drop More Crop
- **Objective**: Enhancing water use efficiency through Micro Irrigation technologies (Drip and Sprinkler irrigation).
- **Subsidies**:
  - Small & Marginal Farmers: Up to 55% financial assistance for installing Drip/Sprinkler systems.
  - Other Farmers: Up to 45% financial assistance.
  - Additional state top-up subsidies are available in states like Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Andhra Pradesh (up to 70-90%).

---

## 4. Soil Health Card Scheme (SHC)
- **Objective**: Promotes soil test-based balanced fertilizer application.
- **Details**: Provides farmers with a report card of 12 soil parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) and tailored fertilizer and organic manure recommendations for each field every 2 years.

---

## 5. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme
- **Objective**: Adequate and timely credit support from the banking system for agricultural operations and allied activities.
- **Interest Subvention**: Concessional loan up to ₹3 Lakh at an effective interest rate of 4% per annum (7% nominal minus 3% prompt repayment incentive). Collateral-free loan limit is ₹1.6 Lakh.
